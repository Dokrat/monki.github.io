Introduction

GymWorkoutTimer ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What Data Do We Collect?

  • Device ID (10 digits) - Only when you request an unlock code
  • Email address - Only to send unlock codes and respond to support
  • Workout data - Stored locally on your device only, never sent to our servers
  • Basic analytics - Only if you visit our website

2. How Do We Use Your Data?

  • Device ID: To verify unlock codes and provide app access
  • Email: To send unlock codes and respond to your inquiries
  • Workout data: Stored only on your device - we never access it

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely. We do not share your personal information with third parties. Device IDs and emails are stored only as long as needed to provide unlock services.

4. Data Retention

We retain your email and Device ID only for the purpose of providing unlock access. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights (RODO/GDPR)

Under RODO (Polish GDPR implementation), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at monki.appps@gmail.com

6. Cookies & Tracking

The website uses minimal tracking. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.

8. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or questions, contact: monki.appps@gmail.com
